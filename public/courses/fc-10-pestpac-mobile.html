Why this course matters

If it isn't posted in PestPac, it didn't happen.

"You are expected to log in to each appointment upon arrival at the customers' property, fill out relevant notes during the service, and log out of the appointment upon completion of the work before physically moving to the next stop."CLEMENTS EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK · POSTING WORK TO MOBILE APP

It is the legal record

Florida rule 5E-14.142 requires pest control records to be recorded within two working days, kept at least two years and shown to FDACS inspectors on request. Your posted order is that record.

It is the customer's report

Customers with an email on file get an inspection report with your notes after every service. It is often the only thing they read from us.

It is proof of the work

Start and finish times verify you were there. Billing, callbacks, complaints and your own defense all depend on what you posted.

01 · Set up and stay secure

The app goes where you go. So does the responsibility.

Get set up

  • Install "PestPac Mobile" from the App Store or Google Play, on a company phone or your own
  • Your supervisor arranges your PestPac account and sign-in
  • Turn on location services so the map and directions work
  • Keep the app and phone updated

Your sign-in is yours

  • Never share it or sign in for someone else. Every order posted under it is recorded as your work
  • Screen lock on, phone never left in view in the truck
  • The app holds gate codes, pets and schedules. Lost phone? Tell your supervisor immediately

Never while driving

  • No texting, calling, browsing or app use while the truck is moving
  • Set the next stop's directions before you drive, or adjust them only when safely stopped
  • Calls go to voicemail; return them when parked
Personal phone used for work? The same professional and security standards apply as on a company phone (Mobile Device Policy). CW-10 covers passwords and phishing.
02 · Start of day and sync

Before the first stop

  1. 1Log in with a good signal (shop or home Wi-Fi is ideal) so the day's orders and any office changes download. sync timing: typical
  2. 2Read the Order List. Every stop, in time order. Orders without a time sit at the top.
  3. 3Read the account notes and instructions for every order: gate codes, pets, customer preferences, last visit's findings.
  4. 4Check the calendar for past dates. No order should be sitting unserviced on an earlier day.
  5. 5Check your truck against the day: products and equipment for every service on the list.

03 · Products and amounts: what Florida requires

Every product, every amount, every service

Florida rule 5E-14.142 · F.A.C.
  • Pest control records are kept for every licensee's work, including contracts
  • For restricted-use pesticides the record must show the brand name, amount, use, date and location
  • Recorded no later than two working days after the application
  • Kept at least two years (three for preventive termite treatment in new construction), electronic records included, and produced for FDACS on request
Clements standard: record every product this way on every service, restricted-use or not. Recording it at the stop is easier than proving it later.

04 · Pests, areas, photos and payment

Specific beats general, every time

Target pests

Name what you treated for: "German cockroach", "fire ants", "spiders". Not "bugs" or "general".

Areas treated

Where it went: "exterior perimeter, kitchen baseboards, garage". Never "everywhere". Note areas skipped and why.

Photos

Attach to the order: conducive conditions, damage, a damaged station, a locked gate. Never people. Never on social media. attachments: typical

Payment

If you take payment at the stop, use only the app's approved process. Never write, text or photograph card numbers.

Products, pests and areas belong in the structured fields where your screen has them, not only in the notes. The fields are what reports, audits and the next technician rely on.

01 · Notes the customer reads

Activities, then the Notes icon, then Save

Start with a preloaded note. Tap the Star icon and pick the one that matches the service. It keeps wording and grammar consistent.
Then add today's specifics the preloaded note can't know: what you found, what you recommend, what happens next, who you spoke with.
Tap Save. If you leave without saving, the notes are erased.
Write for the customer. These notes go on the emailed inspection report. Nothing about the office, coworkers, pricing or complaints.
Anatomy of a good note · same as CW-04
  1. 1What you did, and where
  2. 2What you found: activity, conducive conditions
  3. 3What the customer can do
  4. 4Follow-up: next visit, callback or office action
  5. 5Who you spoke with, or the door hanger left

Plain, professional, factual. No slang, no ALL CAPS, no blame.

03 · Inspection report, signature and completion

The last screen is the one the customer sees

Review

On the Completion page, check products, amounts, pests, areas and notes. This is your last easy chance to fix them.

Signature

If the customer is home and your screen asks, have them sign. Not home? Don't sign for them; note it. typical

Complete

Tap Completion. The order posts, leaves your Order List and syncs to the office.

Report sent

Customers with an email on file get the inspection report automatically, with your notes.

No email on file? The customer gets no report. If they'd like one, send the request to technotes@ so customer service can update the account.
You can't unpost it yourself. Completed the wrong order or missed something? Call or email the office right away for an unpost.

No signal, wrong order, bad data

Why it matters: things will go wrong in the field. A dead zone or a wrong tap is a small problem. A missing, guessed or altered record is a big one, for the customer, the company and your license.

01 · No signal

Dead zone? Keep working, keep recording.

  1. 1Start the order as normal. The app keeps what you enter on the phone until it can sync. offline behavior: typical
  2. 2Record everything at the stop: products, amounts, pests, areas, notes. Don't save it for later.
  3. 3Don't delete, reinstall or log out while orders are waiting to sync. Unsynced work can be lost.
  4. 4Back in coverage, confirm it synced before the end of the day.
  5. 5App down completely? Write it on paper with the real times, tell the office, and enter it as soon as you can. Well inside two working days.

03 · Accurate, complete, true

Your posted record is a legal document

Never
  • Post a service, product or area you didn't do
  • Copy last visit's products or notes without checking them against today
  • Guess an amount, or round it to look right
  • Start or complete orders in a batch at the end of the day
  • Change a record to make a visit look better than it was
  • Post work under someone else's sign-in, or let them use yours
The law: violating Chapter 482 or its rules, or failing to give FDACS true information about the materials used and work performed, are grounds for license discipline (s. 482.161, F.S.).
The company: falsified records are misconduct under the handbook, up to termination.
Made a mistake? That's normal. Tell the office the same day so it is corrected properly. Honest corrections are never the problem.
