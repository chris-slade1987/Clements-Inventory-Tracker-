Documentation & Return Visits

The Rodent Shield record that backs every warranty, the five follow-up visits that finish the job, and how to know when it is done.

Course objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

Build a complete Rodent Shield record: diagram, entry-point log, photos, materials, trap log and notes
Photograph every entry point to the three-photo standard, before and after, and label it to match the log
Keep customer-facing notes, technotes@ messages and internal records in the right place
Explain the five follow-up visits and run every return visit the same way: check, reset, re-inspect, record
Handle a no-access visit without losing the record or the customer's trust
Decide when a job is ready to close, when it is not, and record it correctly in PestPac
Why this course matters

A job that isn't documented isn't covered.

Clements can't back a warranty claim on an entry point nobody photographed. The record you make on day one is what every later visit, renewal and dispute is measured against.

It is the warranty

On Plus, the documented entry points are the scope of the warranty. Sealed but not photographed means it can't be verified at renewal.

It is the legal record

Any rodenticide you place is a pesticide application. Florida rule 5E-14.142 expects the record within two working days, kept at least two years.

It is the hand-off

The tech who checks traps isn't always the one who set them. A clear trap log means the next visit starts where the last one ended.

02 · The inspection diagram

One sketch, one numbering system. Select a marker.

Select a marker on the diagram

EP markers are entry points. T markers are traps and stations. The numbers match the logs, the photo labels and the entry point PDF.

Mark north or the street side, every entry point, every trap and station, and conditions like a palm on the roof. Never renumber on a later visit: new finds get the next number.

03 · The three-photo rule

Every entry point: wide, approach, close-up. Before and after.

Scale in every close-up. A ruler parallel to the opening, or a coin beside it. Without a size reference the photo proves nothing.
Timestamp on, in focus, lit with your flashlight. Never delete a before photo. Same spot, same angle for the after.

02 · Set expectations

Tell the customer the plan before you leave day one

When we're back

Give the cadence up front: the first week is the busy window, then the visits spread out. Put the next visit date in your notes every time.

What we need

Access to the attic hatch and every trap location. Gate codes, pets and who will be home go to the office so the next visit isn't a no-access.

What not to do

Don't move or reset traps, and don't open bait stations. Call us if you hear new activity or a trap goes off. Keep children and pets away from devices.

Who comes back may change. At some branches a different technician runs the follow-ups. Your record is how they know what you did.
Visits beyond five are billed. Never quote the charge or promise extra visits. Pricing questions go to the branch manager.
03 · Every return visit, step by step

Same eight steps, every follow-up

1

Read the last record

Diagram, trap log and notes in PestPac before you knock. Start the order on arrival.

2

Ask the customer

Noises, droppings, smells since last time? Where and when?

3

Check every device

In log order, PPE on. Record each result, including untouched.

4

Remove captures

Wet with disinfectant, double-bag, dispose. Never handle bare-handed. RX-08 covers the method.

5

Reset or move

Reset, re-bait per label, or move toward fresh activity. Log the new spot.

6

Re-inspect the seals

Every sealed EP: intact, gnawed or breached? After photo if anything changed.

7

Look for new activity

Fresh droppings, rub marks, new gaps. New find gets the next EP number.

8

Record and report

Captures, new activity, seals holding, next visit. Complete before you drive.

06 · Re-inspect the seals

Is the exclusion holding? Check every sealed entry point.

Intact

Holding

Cement sound, no gnawing, no fresh rub marks. Note "EP-1 to EP-4 intact" in the log.

Pressure

Gnawed, not through

Rodents are testing it. Photograph, repair, log the repair and keep a trap on that route.

Breached

Open again

Photograph from the original spot, reseal, log it. On Plus, tell your branch manager: it may be warranty work.

Plus · warranty review is triggered when
  • Activity continues in treated areas
  • A missed entry point turns up within the original scope
  • The customer reports new activity matching the original service area
Declined points aren't covered. They're on the service agreement with the homeowner's acknowledgment. Check it before you call something a failure.
Vents stay open. Dryer and exhaust vents are never sealed or screened, even when the customer asks. Note their condition only.

02 · No-access visits

Nobody home, gate locked. Prove you came, then get back fast.

Start the order

On arrival, as always. The start time is your proof of the attempt.

Knock, then call

From the parked truck. No answer? Call the tech line for help reaching them.

Do what you can reach

Exterior stations you can access. Photo of the locked gate or hatch you couldn't.

Record, reschedule

Door hanger, a factual note, and technotes@ to rebook the soonest date.

Unchecked traps can't wait long. A capture left in a Florida attic decays fast and brings odor and flies. Ask for the earliest slot, not the next convenient one.
Does it count as one of the five? Not your call. Note what was and wasn't checked; the branch manager decides.

02 · Closing the job

The last visit leaves a complete record and a clear next step

At the final visit
  1. 1Account for every device on the trap log: removed, or left in place and recorded
  2. 2Final check and photos of every sealed entry point
  3. 3Walk the customer through the results and leave the post-service summary (Basic) or documentation package (Plus)
  4. 4Offer deodorization and exterior rodent boxes as leads, never with a price
Plus: explain what the warranty covers (the documented, sealed entry points), that declined points aren't covered, and that the office reaches out before renewal.
Basic: if entry points were found, present Plus before you leave. Basic has no exclusion and no warranty.
Leads and changes go to technotes@: upgrade interest, add-ons, rodent box refill schedule, anything the office must act on.
03 · Recording in PestPac

Photos, fields and notes on the order, before you leave

  1. 1Start the order on arrival and open last visit's notes and attachments
  2. 2Attach photos to the order, labeled to match: "EP-3 Foundation NE corner · Before"
  3. 3Materials fields: every rodenticide by brand name, amount and location
  4. 4Note: activity level (none, light, moderate, heavy), captures, seals, next visit
  5. 5Check every thumbnail uploaded, then complete before you drive
