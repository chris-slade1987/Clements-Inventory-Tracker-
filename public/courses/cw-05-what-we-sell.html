Why this course matters

Customers ask whoever is in front of them.

A technician at a GHP stop gets asked about termites. A CSR taking a billing call hears about rats in the attic. A salesperson on a lawn estimate is asked about mosquitoes. Every one of us needs to know the whole menu.

A missed question is a missed customer

If you can't explain a service, the customer calls someone who can. Knowing the lineup turns a passing question into a scheduled inspection.

What you say becomes a promise

Florida law requires termite warranties to be spelled out in writing, and pesticide claims must match the label. A careless "it's guaranteed" or "it's totally safe" can put the company at legal risk.

The right fix the first time

Baiting outside a home that still has open entry points can leave a dead rodent in the wall. Matching the problem to the right service protects the customer and our name.

01 · Know the termite

The service depends on which termite it is

Subterranean (including Formosan)

Nest in the soil and travel up through mud tubes. Signs: mud tubes on the foundation, swarmers, damaged wood. Treated with Sentricon or liquid.

Drywood

Live inside the wood, with no soil contact. Signs: small piles of pellets (frass) and tiny exit holes. Treated with drywood spot or whole-home.

Sentricon never treats drywood termites. And we don't offer tent fumigation: Clements does not perform, sell or refer it.
Our termite services

  • Drywood spot treatmentFoam injection in one small, defined area.
  • Drywood whole-homeBora-Care on all accessible wood, preventative or corrective.

02 · Sentricon Always Active

The bait that termites take home to the colony

  1. 1Stations go in the soil around the home, baited (noviflumuron) from day one.
  2. 2Termites prefer the bait to wood and share it through the colony, queen included.
  3. 3The colony is eliminated and the stations keep protecting. We check them quarterly, plus an annual inspection.
The $1,000,000 damage bond is Corteva's, not a Clements warranty. It covers new subterranean damage after installation, only while annual monitoring is current (a lapse past the 30-day grace period voids it), never existing damage. Clements adds no-charge retreatment on top.

Only a Certified Sentricon Specialist (or someone they supervise) sells and services Sentricon. Damage claims go to your branch manager.

04 · WDO inspections and termite warranties

Inspections are regulated. So are the words "warranty" and "guarantee."

WDO real estate inspections

WDO means wood-destroying organisms: termites, wood-decaying fungi and wood-boring beetles. Buyers, sellers and lenders order a WDO inspection during a home sale. The results go on the state form FDACS-13645, and only a WDO ID cardholder or WDO certified operator may perform one. Reports are kept at least three years.

WDO inspection policy and pricing are still being finalized. Take the details and route the request. Don't quote a price or a turnaround time.
Every termite contract states one of these, in bold, on page one (Florida Statute 482.227)
For Repair and RetreatmentThe company retreats and repairs new damage.
For Retreatment OnlyThe company retreats but does not repair damage. All of our drywood work is retreatment only.
No Warranty or GuaranteeNo warranty on the work performed.

If a contract has any conditions or exclusions, it may never use "full" or "unlimited" with "warranty" or "guarantee." The written contract is the promise, so point customers to it.

01 · Rodents

The most important rodent question

"Are you hearing or seeing rodents inside your home, or only outside?"

Exclusion before baiting

Rodents use openings to carry food into attics. If we put out bait stations while openings are still there, a rodent can eat the bait, get back inside and die in a wall. That means odor, health risk and an unhappy customer. Seal first, then bait.

    02 · The Rodent Shield program

    Two tiers, and only one carries a warranty

    Rodent Shield Basic

    Elimination

    • Full interior and exterior inspection
    • Traps and enclosed bait stations in activity areas
    • Five follow-up visits to check traps and remove rodents
    • Written summary of findings for the homeowner
    • No exclusion or sealing work
    • No warranty and no renewal

    Fits when no structural entry points are found, or the customer wants to start with elimination.

    Rodent Shield Plus

    Elimination and exclusion

    • Every entry point ½ inch or larger documented and photographed
    • Entry points sealed with approved materials, cement first
    • Traps placed at the entry points, five follow-up visits
    • Documentation package for the homeowner
    • 1-year or 3-year warranty, chosen at the original service
    • Warranty transfers to a buyer if the home sells (within 30 days)

    If a Basic inspection finds entry points, the technician presents Plus before leaving.

    Add-ons: deodorization, A/C chase sealing and exterior rodent bait boxes. Bait boxes are required to renew a Plus warranty. Foam is not exclusion; rodents chew through it. The 3-year warranty can't be added later. Residential accounts need a credit card deposit before scheduling; commercial accounts do not.
    03 · Mosquito misting

    An automatic system that treats the yard at dawn and dusk

    Standard program: two short cycles a day, about 7:00 am and 7:00 pm, when mosquitoes are most active. The customer can trigger an extra cycle with a remote before guests arrive.

    We install and service MistAway systems, a line that came with the Knockout acquisition. Refill service runs about every quarter, with nozzle and pressure checks.

    Two concentrates, same price to the customer

    Warranty: 12 months on new parts we install and the related labor, plus MistAway's 1-year parts warranty. Not covered: landscaper damage, lines cut by other contractors, or refills and setting changes outside our service.

    01 · Commercial service

    Pest control for businesses

    Restaurants, offices, warehouses, retail and multi-family properties. The pests are familiar; the setting is not. A business's reputation and inspections ride on our work.

    Around the business

    Service is planned around operating hours, deliveries and food prep, so customers and employees aren't disrupted.

    Records that hold up

    Food businesses are inspected by the state. Accurate service records, products used and findings support the account when an inspector asks.

    Prevention first

    Monitoring, sanitation and exclusion recommendations matter as much as treatment. We report conditions the business needs to fix.

    The right contact

    We work through the owner, property manager or facilities contact, who may not be the person on site.

    Multi-unit buildings

    Rodent work in duplexes, quads and apartments goes to a manager first. Sealing one unit doesn't work without a firewall between units.

    Same services, adapted

    Rodent, termite, mosquito and wildlife work all apply to businesses. Commercial rodent accounts don't need a deposit.

    02 · Where the question goes

    Explain the service. Leave the price to the quote.

    Don't quote prices, even a ballpark. Pricing depends on the inspection, the size of the home and the program. Sales courses cover pricing for the people who quote.
    Do explain what the service is, how it works and how often we come, in plain language.
    Do capture what the customer is seeing, where, and for how long, and whether they own the property. Then tell them who will follow up.
