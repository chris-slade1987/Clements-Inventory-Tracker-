Why this course matters

A cleanout is a promise to get their home back.

These customers are bitten, embarrassed or scared. They have emptied cabinets, washed every sheet and left the house for us. If the job fails, they did all of that for nothing.

Health is on the line

Roach droppings and shed skins trigger asthma and allergies, especially in children. In Florida, the brown dog tick carries canine ehrlichiosis and babesiosis.

Inside occupied homes

Beds, couches, kitchens, homes with infants and pets. Every application follows the label: site, rate, method and re-entry. The label is the law (FC-02).

One visit is never the whole job

Flea pupae and roach egg cases shrug off treatment. Without a follow-up and the customer's help, the second wave hatches right on schedule.

02 · Why pupae survive

The cocoon is a waiting room

The larva spins a sticky silk cocoon that picks up carpet fibers and dirt. Inside, the flea finishes developing and then waits, sometimes for months, until vibration or the carbon dioxide of a passing person or pet tells it a meal is near.

  • Sprays and IGRs don't reach it. The cocoon shields the pupa, and an IGR has nothing left to stop: it is already becoming an adult.
  • So we leave a residual and come back. New adults hatch onto treated carpet and die. The follow-up catches the stragglers.
  • Vacuuming helps twice. It removes eggs and flea dirt, and the vibration coaxes pupae out into the residual.
03 · The flea cleanout, step by step

Prep confirmed, everything treated, pets handled the same day

  1. 1Confirm the prep. Bedding washed, floors and closets cleared, vacuumed and mopped, fish tank covered, pets out. Not done? Call your supervisor before you treat.
  2. 2Confirm it's fleas. Ask where the bites happen and where the pet sleeps. Look for flea dirt in pet bedding.
  3. 3Treat every floor, bed and piece of furniture with an adulticide plus IGR, per the label. Hit pet resting spots, under furniture and closet floors.
  4. 4Light space treatment of infested rooms only where the guideline calls for it and the label allows it.
  5. 5Set re-entry and the follow-up before you leave.
The pet is the source. Fleas lay eggs on the animal, and the eggs fall wherever it goes. The customer books a vet flea treatment for the same day as our service. We never treat the animal.
Re-entry: people out at least 4 hours (6 for infants and anyone with breathing problems), pets out 24 hours, and everyone off treated surfaces until dry. If the label says longer, the label wins.
Products: the flea guideline names Ultracide (adulticide with an IGR) and CB-80 for light fogging. Use what your supervisor issues, and only where the label lists the site and the pest.

01 · Florida's ticks

Five you'll meet. Only one moves in.

Select a tick

Four live outdoors and hitch a ride in. The fifth can finish its whole life cycle in a living room.

Tick-borne disease is a medical question. If a customer or a pet has a bite with a rash, fever or illness, tell them to call their doctor or vet. Don't diagnose.

Sources: UF/IFAS Pest Alert, Common Florida Ticks; UF/IFAS EDIS, Brown Dog Tick (IN378).

02 · The tick treatment

Inside, outside and on the dog, at the same time

Inside

Brown dog ticks climb, then hide to molt and lay eggs in cracks, behind baseboards and trim, around window and door frames, and where the dog rests. Treat floors, furniture and beds, and baseboards and cracks, per the label.

Yard harborage

Dog runs, kennels and the dog's favorite shady spots, along fences, under decks, leaf litter and tall grass at wooded edges. Ask the customer to keep grass and weeds short.

The dog, by the vet

A dog is needed to keep an indoor population going. The vet treats the dog the same day, and the customer checks pets after kennels, groomers and dog parks. We never treat the animal.

Why it comes back: each stage can survive 3 to 5 months without feeding, and ticks hide while molting or laying eggs, so a home can look clear and then flare up. A female can lay about 4,000 eggs.
Products: the tick guideline names Nyguard on floors, furniture and beds, and a Suspend plus Precor mix on baseboards, with a B&G sprayer. Confirm the label lists ticks for each product you use. Florida brown dog ticks show permethrin resistance.

German roach cleanouts

Why it matters: the German roach is the hardest household pest we treat. It breeds fast, hides in the tightest cracks, learns to avoid bad baits, and shrugs off the wrong sprays. Spray-and-go loses. A plan wins.

01 · Why German roaches are hard

The female carries her eggs until they hatch

Eggs per case30–40The ootheca rides on the female, protected, until just before hatch.
Egg to adultabout 100 daysNymphs mature in about 60 days at room temperature. Warm kitchens speed it up.
Nymphs in a populationabout 80%Most of what you're fighting is young roaches that haven't bred yet.
GenerationsoverlappingFemales breed continuously. Growth is exponential.
Sprays miss the eggs. Kill the adults and the egg cases hatch anyway. That's the second wave.
Bait aversion. Some populations have learned to avoid certain bait ingredients. Rotate bait formulations when acceptance drops.
Repellency scatters them. Repellent sprays push roaches deeper into walls and into the next unit.

Biology: UF/IFAS EDIS, German Cockroach (IN128). ID and harborage: PC-07 Pest ID: Ants, Roaches, Rodents.

Put a German roach cleanout in order

Select the steps in the order you'd do them in a heavily infested kitchen.

03 · Bait, IGR and monitors

Three tools that work together, and one that ruins them

Gel bait

Many small placements, pea-size or smaller as the label directs, in cracks at harborage: hinges, corners, behind and under appliances, plumbing gaps. Roaches eat it and carry it back.

IGR

Stops nymphs from becoming breeding adults. It doesn't kill fast, but it breaks the next generation. Our roach guideline pairs Gentrol with the crack-and-crevice work.

Monitors

Sticky monitors under the sink, behind the fridge and stove. They show where roaches are and give you a count to compare at the follow-up.

Repellent spray

Pyrethroid sprays, pyrethrin aerosols and fogs repel roaches. Never apply them on or near bait. Contaminated bait gets ignored.

Bait first, spray last. If the job calls for a residual or space treatment, keep it out of the cracks you bait. Bait goes in clean, untreated harborage.
Old dried bait is no longer eaten. Remove it, and rotate to a different bait formulation when roaches stop taking it.

05 · Apartments and multi-unit

Roaches don't stop at the unit line

Check the neighbors. Shared walls, plumbing chases and electrical runs connect units. Inspect units above, below and beside an infested one, as the property allows.
Don't push them next door. A repellent spray in one unit can drive roaches into the next. Bait-led treatment keeps them home to eat.
Fill in the Cleanout Tracking Sheet unit by unit: vacant, roaches seen, follow-up needed, housekeeping notice left, cabinets cleaned out, notes.
Work through the property manager. Prep notices, access and units that need a housekeeping notice go to the manager. Residents get the prep guide (English and Spanish versions exist).

04 · Documentation

If it isn't written down, the next visit starts from zero

PestPac Mobile, every visit. Start the order on arrival. Record the pest, rooms treated, each product and amount, and monitor counts. Notes print on the customer's report, so write them for the customer (FC-10).
Record the prep status honestly. "Cabinets not emptied; minimum crack-and-crevice treatment only" protects the customer, you and the company.
Confirm the follow-up date is on the schedule before you leave, and tell the customer what to expect until then.
Anything the office must act on (a reschedule, extra units, prep not done, a pricing question) goes to technotes@clementspestcontrol.com, not in the customer note.

German cockroach activity found in kitchen and hall bath: live roaches, droppings and egg cases behind the refrigerator and in sink cabinet hinges.

Treated cracks and crevices with IGR, placed gel bait in cabinet hinges, appliance gaps and plumbing gaps, and set 4 monitors. Please leave bait and monitors in place, and keep counters clear of food.

Stay out 4 hours. Follow-up scheduled within 7–10 business days. A leak under the kitchen sink was noted; fixing it will help.
