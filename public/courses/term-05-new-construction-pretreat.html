Why this course matters

Once the concrete is poured, there is no second chance.

A pretreat goes under a slab nobody will see again. If it's skipped, diluted by rain or torn up by the plumber, the family who buys that house inherits the risk, and Florida is one of the highest termite-pressure states in the country.

No certificate, no occupancy

The Florida Building Code requires termite protection on new buildings. The building department won't sign off until our Certificate of Compliance is in.

Some of the strictest rules we work under

FDACS Rule 5E-14 sets how the product is mixed, how much goes down, what we record and how long we keep it. Inspectors can and do audit pretreat records.

Builders hire the crew they trust

Superintendents run tight schedules. Showing up when the slab is ready, treating right and turning the paperwork around fast is how we keep the account.

01 · Two paths, one goal

Any registered method meets the code. We lead with Sentricon.

Clements default

Sentricon Always Active

  • Stations installed at perimeter completion, after final grade and landscaping
  • No slab soil treatment or trenching; nothing to wash out
  • Corteva's damage bond (not a Clements warranty) from install: new subterranean damage, while annual monitoring is current (30-day grace)
  • Coded SEN-NEW INST in PestPac, builder accounts only
Builder-specified only

Liquid soil pretreat

  • When the builder's contract or financing (HUD, VA, a lender) calls for it
  • Termiticide applied to the soil before the slab is poured, then a final grade treatment outside
  • Staged: often two or more visits, not one
  • Coded T-SOIL (slab) and T-FINAL (final grade)
Optional companion: borate on bare framing after dry-in, before drywall. It covers drywood termites and decay fungi that Sentricon doesn't. Check with your branch manager first.
The talking track: do the pretreat the builder expects, every time. The branch manager or sales presents Sentricon as the upgrade for their buyers. Never quote a price.
02 · From the call to the job

What has to be in place before you roll

  1. 1Builder account: company, billing contact, and the master agreement for multi-lot builders
  2. 2Job site: exact address and lot number, plus the superintendent's phone
  3. 3Permit number: mandatory, in the order notes before treatment
  4. 4Size and scope: square footage of the slab or pour, and what it is (home, addition, porch)
  5. 5Ready date: when the site will be graded, compacted and plumbed
Who can treat: a certified operator in the termite and WDO category, or an employee with an FDACS identification card working under one (s. 482.091). Sentricon installs need a Certified Sentricon Specialist or a tech they supervise.
Scheduling and reporting run through Routing & Logistics (Jessica Sanderson). Pricing and builder terms come from your branch manager. Office follow-ups go to technotes@clementspestcontrol.com.
Attached to an existing house? The Florida new-construction warranty rule only covers new construction that doesn't attach to an existing structure (5E-14.105(3)(d)). An addition is a different contract: route it to your branch manager.

02 · The label and the mixing rules

Rule 5E-14.106: four lines every pretreat tech knows

Label rate, every time

Apply the exact amount, concentration and treatment areas the label designates for pre-construction (5E-14.106(6)).

Mix at the site

From the original formulation, at the treatment site, immediately before you apply it. No pre-mixed tanks from the shop (5E-14.106(7)).

Label in the vehicle

A copy of the registered label rides in the truck you apply from (5E-14.106(8)).

A second product is allowed

After a full primary treatment, a second product labeled for new construction may be added per its label (5E-14.106(9)).

Example only · Termidor SC (EPA Reg. 7969-210) pre-construction directions
0.06% finished dilution: 0.8 fl oz per gallon of water. Horizontal barrier: 1 gallon per 10 sq ft (coarse fill such as washed gravel takes more; see label). Vertical barrier: 4 gallons per 10 linear ft per foot of depth. Always read the label on the container you're using.
Field Core still applies: concentrates locked in an unattended truck, anti-siphon fill with a 2 inch air gap, PPE per label (FC-02, FC-03, FC-05). Respect label setbacks from wells and water, and never let runoff leave the pad.
03 · Practice · volume calculator

Measure it, calculate it, record it

Uses the Termidor SC example rates. The numbers you calculate on site are the numbers you record: total square footage, product, percent concentration and total volume (5E-14.106(8)).
04 · Final grade and Sentricon at completion

The last step outside, after the landscapers leave

Final grade treatment (T-FINAL)

  • After construction is complete, including initial landscaping and irrigation (R318.1.6)
  • Concrete overpour and mortar along the foundation removed first (R318.1.5)
  • Soil within 1 foot of the sidewalls and under exterior concrete treated; vertical barrier at the foundation
  • Soil disturbed afterward is retreated
  • Required before final building approval when soil treatment is the method (FBC 105.10)

Sentricon at perimeter completion

  • Installed before final building approval (R318.1.7), once grade and landscaping are done so stations aren't buried or dug up
  • Clements standard (TERM-02): about 10 ft apart (label maximum 20 ft), 2 to 3 ft off the foundation (never closer than 18 in to liquid-treated soil), 10 to 12 in deep, flush with grade
  • Station count, linear footage and map uploaded to PestPac
  • Photograph every station and the perimeter as installed
Clements practice (CSR process): we perform a final only where we did the initial pretreat. If another company did the slab, route the request to your branch manager.
Point out conducive conditions to the builder now: condensate lines, sprinkler risers and downspouts must discharge at least 1 foot from the wall (R318.5).
