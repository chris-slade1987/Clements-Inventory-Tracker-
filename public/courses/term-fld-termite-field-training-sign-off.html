Why this course matters

Termite work is judged for years.

A GHP mistake shows up next week. A termite mistake can show up years later as structural damage, a denied claim or a state investigation. The paperwork you leave today is what defends the customer, the bond and our license then.

The most regulated thing we do

Chapter 482 and rule 5E-14 set termite contracts, notices, reports, treatment standards and record retention. Termite and WDO is its own category: GHP and L&O days don't count toward it.

A $1M bond rides on your install

Corteva's damage bond depends on installs that follow the label and stewardship program, and on Clements keeping its Certified Sentricon Specialist standing. Your station map and photos are the bond's evidence.

It's the customer's biggest investment

Homeowner's insurance generally doesn't cover termite damage. Customers trust us to find what's there, treat it properly and tell them the truth about what we can and can't reach.

01 · What Florida requires

Two rules, two minimums, in the termite category

Statute · Fla. Stat. 482.091(3)

At least 5 days of field training

  • Before you perform, solicit, inspect or apply on your own
  • Under the direct supervision, direction and control of a certified operator
  • In the appropriate category. Termites and other wood-destroying organisms is its own category
Rule · FAC 5E-14.1421

40 hours, documented

  • The initial five-day training is 40 hours
  • Given by a certified operator, or a trainer the certified operator in charge designates in writing
  • Verified on form FDACS-13665 (Verification Record of Initial Employee Training) or an equivalent attendance record, a separate one for termite
  • Kept by Clements for at least 2 years
5termite days minimum. Long days don't replace the fifth day.
40termite hours is the state floor. Clements' own termite target is still being set and may be higher.
0GHP or L&O hours that transfer. Each category has its own days and its own record.
02 · Practice

Does it count toward your termite hours?

Supervised termite and WDO training counts. Breaks, online courses, solo work and other categories don't. Clements makes the final call on edge cases, so ask if you're unsure.

03 · Three credentials, three keys

Field training is one key. These are the others.

FDACS employee ID card

Clements applies within 30 days of hire; HR starts it on Day 1. Carry it and show it on request (482.091).

Special WDO ID card

Required to perform WDO inspections (482.091(9)). Its affidavit, signed by you and the certified operator in charge, swears you're trained in WDO ID, the report form and the law. You work under a WDO-category certified operator.

Certified Sentricon Specialist

Corteva's certification, earned through Sentricon University online in about 3–4 hours (TERM-02). Only a CSS, or someone a CSS supervises, may install or service stations.

Field training grants neither. Finish every termite day and you still can't do solo Sentricon work without your CSS, or any WDO inspection without the WDO card.
Florida also requires the WDO card for contract inspections and treatments (482.226(3)(b)). Your branch manager confirms which cards you need.

03 · Monitoring and active accounts

An install is only as good as the visits that follow

Quarterly

Station monitoring

  • Open every mapped station: bait, activity, integrity
  • Replace consumed bait as the label directs, and any damaged station
  • Note obstructions; ask the customer to clear them
  • Photos and the station log in PestPac Mobile
Annual

Full inspection

  • Doubles as the formal Corteva label inspection
  • Inspect the structure as well as the stations
  • Leave the signed report: termites present or absent, treated or not, product used (482.226(3))
Active account

Live termites found

  • Re-inspect every 30–60 days until two visits in a row show no activity
  • Above-ground stations over active tubes or feeding sites where the label allows
  • Then back to the standard cycle
The $1M damage bond is Corteva's, not a Clements warranty. It covers new subterranean termite damage after installation while annual monitoring is current (30-day grace). Claims go through the branch manager.
Never call any termite coverage "full" or "unlimited" (482.227), and never promise results. Renewal and pricing questions go to the branch manager (TERM-07).

02 · New-construction pretreat

The rules your lead checks on a builder job

Sentricon is the default

Sentricon Always Active at perimeter completion, to the same label spec as a retrofit. Liquid pretreat only when the builder specifies it (TERM-05).

Mix at the site, label in the truck

Liquid pretreat is mixed at the treatment site immediately before application, and a copy of the label rides in the vehicle (5E-14.106(7)–(8)).

Records kept 3 years

Date, address, total square footage, product, percent concentration and total volume, for every pretreat (5E-14.106(8), 5E-14.142(1)). Ordinary pesticide records are 2 years.

Building code paperwork

A treatment certificate for each completed treatment goes to the jobsite and the building department, and a soil-barrier job's final exterior treatment is done before the building final (Florida Building Code).

Post the treatment notice

At least 3 by 5 inches, lasting 3 years, beside the attic or other accessible area: our name and address, date, product and organism (482.226(5)). Only the owner may remove it.

Warranty set by rule

Preventive new-construction treatment carries at least a 1-year warranty, which the owner can renew annually for at least 4 more years (5E-14.105(3)). You explain it; the branch manager handles terms.

04 · WDO inspections

You watch WDO inspections. You don't do them.

What you observe
  • A WDO cardholder or WDO certified operator inspects every accessible area and records what couldn't be reached
  • Findings go on the state form FDACS-13645, the Wood-Destroying Organisms Inspection Report, with no added disclaimers
  • An inspection notice is posted near the attic or crawl access
  • Clements keeps a copy of the report for 3 years
WDO inspection policy and pricing are still being finalized. If a customer asks for one, take the details and route it to the branch manager. Don't quote a price or a turnaround (TERM-06).
Rules that never bend
  • No WDO card, no WDO inspection, even with your lead beside you to sign (482.091(9))
  • On a GHP visit you never diagnose, quote or promise termite treatment: photos, then the branch manager the same day
  • Only the property owner removes a WDO notice (482.226)
  • Not sure if it's subterranean or drywood? The certified operator decides before anything is sold
Your training days build the knowledge the WDO card affidavit swears to: WDO biology and ID, the report form, and the law.
