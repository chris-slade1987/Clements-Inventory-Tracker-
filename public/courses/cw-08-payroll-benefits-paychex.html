Why this course matters

Your paycheck is your responsibility too.

"Your paycheck is important but it does not represent all of the compensation you receive." Benefits, PTO and retirement are part of what you earn. You only get full value from them if you understand them.

Missed windows cost you

Miss a benefit enrollment deadline and you may wait until the next open enrollment for coverage. Late PTO requests can be denied.

Accurate records are the law

Federal wage law requires accurate time and pay records. Falsifying a time record is misconduct under our handbook.

Errors get fixed faster when caught early

Check every stub. A mistake you report right away is corrected on or before the next payday.

01 · The pay schedule

Bi-weekly, every other Friday, by direct deposit

  • Every two weeks. That is 26 paydays in most years, and 27 in a year that happens to hold an extra Friday.
  • Direct deposit to the account you set up at onboarding. Keep it current, or your pay can be delayed.
  • Your stub posts in Paychex Flex every payday. Open it and check it.

Your supervisor confirms your work schedule. Core office hours are 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.; technician hours follow the route and customer demand.

02 · Recording your time

Your time record is a legal record. Keep it honest.

Use the method for your role

Depending on your position, Clements uses the web clock, time sheets or day sheets. Your supervisor shows you which applies to you in your first week.

Never for someone else

Filling in a time or day sheet for another employee, clocking in for them, or altering your own entry without authorization is misconduct under the handbook.

Fix mistakes the right way

Forgot to clock in, or entered the wrong time? Tell your supervisor the same day so it can be corrected and approved. Don't edit around it.

Federal overtime basics (FLSA): non-exempt employees earn at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Florida adds no daily overtime rule.
No off-the-clock work. If your role is paid by the hour, all time you work counts. Don't start early or finish tasks after you clock out.

04 · Deductions

Why your take-home is less than what you earned

Required by law

  • Federal income tax, based on the Form W-4 you complete. Change it any time with a new W-4.
  • Social Security and Medicare (FICA). You pay one share and Clements pays a matching share.
  • No Florida income tax. Florida has no state personal income tax, so there is no state withholding line.

Your choices and obligations

  • Benefit premiums. Your share of medical, dental and vision is taken before tax (IRS Section 125).
  • 401(k) contributions, if you enroll in the retirement plan.
  • Other authorized deductions, such as voluntary insurance, always itemized on your stub.
Each January you receive a Form W-2 showing your total earnings and taxes withheld for the year. Employers must provide it by January 31.

02 · Your account

Set it up once. Keep it yours.

  1. 1HR sends your invitation during onboarding. Create your own login, then set up direct deposit and review your W-4.
  2. 2Turn on every security option offered, such as a verification code to your phone.
  3. 3Never share your login or let anyone sign in for you. No one at Clements needs your password.
  4. 4Locked out? Use the Paychex "forgot username or password" link first. Still stuck, contact HR.

Tell HR in writing when these change

  • Address or phone number
  • Legal name or marital status
  • Dependents or insurance beneficiaries
  • Emergency contact
  • Bank account for direct deposit

Some changes can also be made in Flex. Either way, HR must know. It keeps your pay, taxes and benefits right.

01 · Your PTO

How much PTO you have, and when

  • Eligible after the 90-day probationary period.
  • Hired January–June: 5 days in your first year. Hired July–December: PTO starts the next calendar year.
  • 10 days a year after that; 15 days at 10 years of service, the maximum.
  • No rollover. Unused PTO does not carry into the next year. Commission technicians may request a payout by email to HR by October 1. Check your balance in Flex.

03 · Holidays and other leave

Paid holidays, and leave when life happens

Offices closed

  • New Year's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Day after Thanksgiving
  • Christmas Eve
  • Christmas Day

Schedule subject to change. Eligibility and pay depend on your pay type; HR can confirm yours.

Bereavement: up to 3 paid days for immediate family, 1 day for extended family or a close relation.
Parental leave: after one year of service. Submit the request form to HR 30 days ahead when possible.
Family and medical leave: talk to your supervisor and HR as soon as you know you'll need it.
Jury duty and military service: give your supervisor a copy of the summons or orders as soon as you receive them.
04 · Benefits

What's offered to regular full-time employees

Medical, dental, vision

Group plans. Per the handbook, Clements pays half the cost of medical and dental; vision is employee-paid. Your share comes out before tax.

Disability and life

Group short- and long-term disability and life insurance, plus voluntary life coverage for you and your dependents at group rates.

401(k) Retirement & Profit Sharing

For eligible employees. Plan details are in the Paychex portal; questions go to HR or the designated Paychex representative.

Who's eligible: regular full-time employees (40 or more hours a week). Part-time and temporary employees are not eligible for most benefits.
The plan documents govern. Summary Plan Descriptions and plan booklets are available from HR. If the handbook and a plan document differ, the plan document wins.

01 · If you leave Clements

Your final paycheck, at a high level

Give notice

Give management at least two weeks' written notice. HR schedules an exit interview, usually on your last day.

When you're paid

Your final pay is direct deposited on the next regular bi-weekly payday after your last day. Need a paper check? Ask HR.

Return company property

Vehicle, keys, phone, devices, card, uniforms. Items not returned or returned damaged may be deducted where the law allows, with an itemized list.

Unused PTO is not paid out when employment ends, under current policy. Plan your time off during the year.
Insurance ends at the end of the month you leave. You can continue it for 18 to 36 months under COBRA; details come by mail.
