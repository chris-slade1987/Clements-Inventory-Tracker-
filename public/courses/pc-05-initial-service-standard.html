Initial Service Standard

The first visit is the one every other visit is measured against. Deliver what was promised at the sale, set the baseline, set honest expectations and leave a record the next technician can use.

45–60 min5 checkpoints20-question check · 80% to passGHP technicians
Course objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

Explain what the customer was promised at the sale, and read the sales turnover before you arrive
Run the initial as a full interior and exterior service, with a customer walk-through and a baseline inspection
Complete the New Customer Start Form with the customer and get it to the office
Give clear prep and safety instructions and an honest knock-down expectation, with no promises
Place the service sticker correctly and write notes the next technician can rely on
Tell the customer how the recurring cycle works and when we'll be back
Why this course matters

The initial is the account.

FC-11 taught the standard visit. This course is the one visit that sets up all the others: the customer just signed, they're watching closely, and everything you see, say and record today becomes the starting point for every technician after you.

It was promised in writing

The customer has a date and a 3:00–5:00 PM window written on their agreement, a confirmation from the office, and a scope the service adviser explained. You are the person who keeps that promise.

It sets the baseline

Every future visit and callback is compared with what you find and write today. Skip the start form or the walk-through and the next technician starts blind.

It decides whether they stay

A customer who wasn't told what to expect sees more bugs in week one and calls to cancel. A customer who was told stays calm, and stays with us.

The initial vs. a recurring visit

Same visit standard. Much more to deliver.

Switch between the two. Everything in FC-11 still applies to both; these are the things that change on the first visit.

01 · The promise, in writing

Next business day, 3:00–5:00 PM. No open-ended starts.

  • Under the New Start policy (effective 9/9/2026), the adviser writes the date and the 3:00–5:00 PM window on the contract and confirms it out loud.
  • Routing puts it on a route and sends the customer a text or email confirmation the same day.
  • The new start is your last stop. Routing assigns no more than one per technician per day and spreads them out.
  • Not home or no access? Routing contacts the customer and reschedules within 48 hours. Technicians don't schedule new starts.

01 · Interior and exterior, every initial

The default is the whole home. Here's what changes it.

Customer home, agrees

Full interior and exterior

Every room on the agreement, including anything the adviser promised, then the full exterior. Never skip the interior because it's faster.

Customer declines the interior

Respect it, note it, keep the door open

Note the decline in PestPac and tell them the interior is included at no extra charge on any future visit. Just ask.

No answer at the door

Every reasonable attempt first

Knock or ring, then call or text from the truck as the account says. Then do the exterior you can reach, leave a completed door hanger and email technotes@ so routing can reschedule the interior within 48 hours.

Adviser did a knockdown at the sale

It's still a full initial

A knockdown is limited work to give immediate relief, such as bait on the specific problem. It is not a partial initial. Do the whole service.

Rule number one inside: shoe covers on as you step in. Never take your shoes off instead. On a first visit, it's one of the first things the customer judges.

05 · Treat it right the first time

The full treatment, aimed at what the walk-through showed you

Interior · PC-02

  • Brief inspection: kitchen, baths, laundry
  • Crack and crevice where pests hide and travel
  • Baseboards, door and window frames, under appliances
  • Pea-size gel bait placements in target areas

Exterior · PC-03

  • Exterior inspection: entry points, moisture, harborage
  • All three passes, in the order and band PC-03 teaches
  • Webs and inactive nests, the liquid perimeter and entry points
  • Granules in beds and mulch, swept off hard surfaces

Only on the initial

  • Start with the customer's problem areas
  • Treat everything the adviser promised, like the garage
  • Leave the adviser's knockdown bait in place
  • Check any bait stations included in the agreement
Never spray liquid over gel bait, yours or the adviser's. It contaminates the bait and pests avoid it.
The label is the law on every product: site, rate, PPE and re-entry (FC-02, FC-03). Mix rates are in PC-04.

01 · Prep and safety instructions

Tell them what to do before, during and after. Select each card.

Never call a product "safe," "harmless" or "non-toxic." Share the label's directions instead.
Use what the start form told you: name the pet, the napping child, the room to avoid. It shows you listened.
02 · The knock-down period

Seeing more bugs after the first treatment is common. Say so.

Say it like this"You may see more activity for several days as they come out and cross the treatment, and the bait takes time to work. If you're still seeing them after that, call the office and we'll come back at no charge."
No promises. Not "gone by tomorrow," "guaranteed" or "you'll never see another one."

04 · The recurring cycle

Before you leave, they should know when we're coming back

  • The initial is scheduled for speed. The recurring service is scheduled for route density. Routing sets the permanent day and time, separately from the initial.
  • You tell the customer their recurring day. Check the account's schedule in PestPac Mobile, or ask the office, before your knock back.
  • Standard GHP is every other month, on the same day and time each visit (CW-05). Schedule codes show where it lands (FC-09).
  • After the initial: exterior every visit, interior on request or when there's activity inside, and callbacks between visits at no charge.

01 · The service sticker

Thirty seconds that can keep the account for years

Every initial gets a Clements service sticker with your name or technician ID, the date and the services performed. When the home sells, the new owner finds it during their walk-through and knows who to call.

At the electrical panel: open the panel door only. Never remove the panel cover or touch anything inside.
Never cover or remove a termite (WDO) inspection or treatment notice. Florida law lets only the property owner remove one (s. 482.226, F.S.). Our sticker is a company standard, not that legal notice.

02 · Notes for the next technician

One visit, three readers

The customer

PestPac notes print on the inspection report they receive: what you treated, what you found, what you recommend and what to expect.

The office

technotes@clementspestcontrol.com: start-form details that change the account, leads, and anything routing must act on, like an interior to reschedule.

The next technician

Reads the account notes the office builds from your form and email, plus your service history. That's why baseline detail matters.

A baseline note that works"Initial service, interior and exterior, garage included. Ghost ants trailing from the kitchen window; treated and baited. Mulch touching the north wall gives them a bridge; please pull it back. Webs removed. Keep pets off treated baseboards until dry. You may see more ants for several days. Service sticker placed inside the electrical panel door."
Keep codes out of customer notes. Gate codes and lockbox numbers go on the start form and to technotes@, not on a report that gets emailed.
