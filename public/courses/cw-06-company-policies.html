Company Policies

The rules every Clements employee works by: respect and how to report a problem, attendance and time off, drugs and alcohol, company property and information, and safe, phone-free driving.

Course objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

Recognize discrimination, harassment and retaliation, and report them through any of the three open doors
Call out correctly and request time off on the right timeline for your role
Explain our drug and alcohol, smoking, weapons and safety-reporting rules
Protect company property, devices and customer information, on the job and online
Follow Florida law and our stricter phone-free driving rule, and handle a vehicle accident
Use the open door and grievance process, and know how progressive discipline works
Why this course matters

Policies protect people first.

"Compliance with this code is the responsibility of every employee of Clements, both with regard to your own conduct, as well as with respect to reporting any possible violations."CLEMENTS EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK 2025 · PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

Lives are on the road

Crashes involving distracted drivers killed 3,208 people on U.S. roads in 2024 (NHTSA). Many of us drive a marked Clements truck all day. One glance at a phone can end a life and a career.

The law backs these rules

Federal and Florida law prohibit discrimination, harassment and retaliation, and Florida traffic law covers phone use behind the wheel. Breaking them carries legal consequences for you and the company.

Customers let us into their homes

They trust us with their property, their schedules and their information. A photo posted online or a careless comment can lose that trust for good.

01 · Equal employment opportunity

Decisions are made on ability, never on who you are

Hiring, pay, benefits, discipline, promotion, termination and every other term of employment are decided without regard to:

If a disability, or pregnancy or a related condition, affects how you do your job, talk to HR. We make reasonable accommodations so qualified people can perform the essential functions of the job.

02 · What harassment looks like

Verbal, physical or visual, in person or on a screen

Select each card. Harassment is prohibited whether it comes from a coworker, a supervisor, a vendor or a customer, at work or at any work-related event.

03 · The reporting path

Three open doors. Pick whichever feels right.

You do not have to go to your supervisor first. Report early. Select each step to see what happens.
04 · Open door and grievances

For every other concern, large or small

Pay questions, scheduling, a policy you think isn't working, a conflict with a coworker: Clements wants to hear it. Nobody is penalized for expressing a point of view.

You may always discuss your own wages, benefits and working conditions with coworkers. Our handbook protects that right under the National Labor Relations Act.
  1. 1Talk with your immediate supervisor. Most problems are solved here.
  2. 2Or any manager or director you feel comfortable with.
  3. 3Not resolved in a reasonable time? Put it in writing to HR.
  4. 4HR reviews the facts and usually answers within five working days. HR's decision is final.

01 · Attendance and calling out

If you can't be there, call. Don't text.

  1. 1Call your supervisor personally, no later than your scheduled start time. Technicians call their Branch Manager. A text or voicemail is not notice.
  2. 2Out more than two days in a row for illness? Bring a doctor's note to return to work.
  3. 3Technicians: call missed accounts within 24 hours and reschedule them within one week. Can't catch up? Tell your supervisor within two business days of returning.
Two consecutive days absent without proper notice and you are subject to termination.
Core hours are 8:00 to 5:00. Your supervisor sets your exact schedule. Customer service is in at 8:00 with phones on at 9:00.
Technician meetings are required. Can't attend? Get your supervisor's approval in advance.
An occasional late arrival from a genuine emergency isn't penalized. A pattern of tardiness or sick days is tracked and leads to discipline.

03 · Drugs and alcohol

Clements is a drug-free workplace

Never use, possess, sell or be under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs, or misuse legal drugs, on company premises, in a company vehicle, or while doing company business.

You must tell management

  • About any prescription that may affect your judgment, driving or behavior, before you work or drive
  • Right away if you are convicted of a drug or alcohol offense
  • If you suspect a coworker or manager is using on the job

When you may be tested

Pre-employmentRandomReasonable suspicionAfter any work accident

After an accident or on reasonable suspicion, you may be suspended without pay until results come back. A positive test is misconduct.

Medical marijuana: a Florida card does not permit use or impairment at work or in a company vehicle. Florida law lets employers keep a drug-free workplace policy.
04 · Smoking, weapons and safety

A safe workplace is everyone's job

Smoking and vaping

Not inside or within 50 feet of office entrances, never in a company vehicle, never at a customer's property.

Weapons

No guns or large knives at work, in offices or in company vehicles. A small pocket knife is fine. Florida law (s. 790.251) allows a lawfully owned firearm kept locked inside your own vehicle in a parking lot; the handbook is being updated to match. Questions go to HR.

Report it now

Any unsafe condition goes to your supervisor right away. Any injury to you, a customer or a visitor goes to management and HR immediately, however small. Work injuries are covered by workers' compensation.

Searches

Desks, lockers, company vehicles, and bags or vehicles on company premises can be searched when property is missing or a violation is suspected. Cooperation is required.

02 · Phones, email and internet

Company systems are company records

No expectation of privacy. Company phones, email, voicemail, computers and accounts can be monitored, accessed or collected by management at any time.
Personal use is fine when it's occasional and doesn't get in the way of work. Keep personal calls brief, ideally on breaks. Phones on silent or vibrate in meetings.
Lost, stolen or damaged company device? Tell your supervisor immediately. Leave company data on it when you leave Clements.

Writing to customers and vendors

Do
  • Proper grammar and punctuation
  • The company signature block
  • Write as if it's on letterhead
Don't
  • ALL CAPS, slang or emoji
  • Offensive or harassing content
  • Open unknown attachments

Don't download games or unapproved software. Unsure about a link or attachment? Ask before you click.

03 · Confidentiality and conflicts

What you see on the job stays on the job

Customer names, addresses, what we do for them and the condition of their homes are confidential. Use them only to do your work.

No photos of customers' homes or businesses on social media, and no posting or chatting about what you saw inside. Even with the address cropped out.

Being confidential about customers and company business never limits your right to talk about your own pay and working conditions.

Non-solicitation agreement

You signed one at hire. Never take a Clements customer's business for yourself or another company. HR has your copy.

Second jobs and conflicts

Discuss any outside job with management first. No side business that competes with Clements or pulls you from your hours.

Speaking for Clements

Only an authorized officer can sign contracts or commit company money. Posting for the company online needs leadership approval.

04 · Conduct and discipline

How progressive discipline works

1

Verbal correction

Your manager talks it through with you. A note goes in your file.

2

Written correction

You sign to show you received it. A copy goes in your file.

3

One-day unpaid suspension

Two write-ups for the same issue, or three for anything, within 12 months.

4

Termination

A fourth write-up within 12 months.

Serious misconduct can skip steps. Management may begin at any step. Employment is at-will.

03 · Vehicle rules and your driving record

Drive like the company name is on the door. It is.

  • Everyone buckles up, every trip. Report a broken seat belt immediately.
  • Other distractions count too: no eating, drinking or reading while driving.
  • Back into parking spaces when possible. Carry your license and the company insurance card.
  • Move Over: Florida law requires you to move over or slow down for any vehicle stopped with its hazard lights on, not just police and tow trucks.

To drive for Clements you must keep

2max standard violations in 3 years
0accidents with a violation in 3 years
0major violations, such as DUI, in 5 years

Driving records are checked periodically.

Tickets in a company vehicle are yours. Turn parking tickets in to HR right away. Speeding, red-light and other moving violations are your responsibility.
04 · If you have an accident in a company vehicle

Stop. Make it safe. Call. Document.

  1. 1Stop at once. Anyone hurt? Call 911 first.
  2. 2Make the scene safe. No injuries and the vehicle can be driven? Florida law (s. 316.061) requires a reasonable effort to move it out of traffic lanes; the handbook is being updated to match. Otherwise leave it until police say to move it.
  3. 3Call the police for every accident, however small, and stay until they release you.
  4. 4Call your supervisor and HR immediately. You'll get instructions for the post-accident drug screen.
  1. 5Photograph all damage and the scene.
  2. 6Collect: other drivers' names, addresses, license and insurance details, and witness names and phone numbers. Fill out the vehicle accident form.
  3. 7Don't discuss fault with anyone except the police.
  4. 8Product spilled? Contain it and follow spill procedures.
