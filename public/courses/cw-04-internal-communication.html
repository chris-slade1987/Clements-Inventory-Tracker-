Internal Communication

Who owns what, how to reach them, how fast to answer, and how to write a tech note or an escalation the office can act on the first time.

40–50 min4 checkpoints20-question check · 80% to pass
Course objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

Route any question to the right person the first time, using the Corporate Contact & Reference Sheet
Follow the escalation path for customer and operational issues without skipping steps
Pick the right channel for the urgency: call, email, tech note or face to face
Meet Clements' response standard: calls and emails answered through the day, nothing left by close of business
Write a clear, accurate tech note and an escalation email the office can act on without calling you back
Keep every message professional, confidential and safe, and handle disagreements the right way
Why this course matters

Every message is part of the service.

The customer never sees the email, the call back or the note in PestPac. They only see the result: a visit that happened when it should, a question answered the first time, a problem fixed without telling their story twice.

"Clear, courteous communication fosters a collaborative work environment and ensures problems are solved efficiently."CLEMENTS ONBOARDING · COMMUNICATION WITH THE OFFICE

Customer trust

A message sent to the wrong person, or answered a day late, becomes a missed service, a wrong bill or a customer who calls back angry. In a hub-and-spoke company, the office and the field only work if information moves between them.

Records the state can inspect

Florida rule 5E-14.142 requires pest control records to be recorded within two working days and kept at least two years for FDACS inspectors. Your PestPac notes and log-in times are those records.

Some clocks are legal deadlines

OSHA requires a work-related death reported within 8 hours; a hospitalization, amputation or eye loss within 24. That only works if you report right away.

03 · Response standards

Answer through the day. Nothing waits past close of business.

Start of dayCheck messages

Read email and voicemail before your first stop or first call. Customer service is in at 8:00; phones open to customers at 9:00.

Through the dayRespond as you go

When the office calls, answer or call back as soon as you can. Reply to email throughout the day, not in one batch at night.

DrivingLet it go to voicemail

No calls, texts or email behind the wheel, hands-free included. Park safely, then respond.

Close of businessClear the list

Every missed call returned and every lingering email answered by end of day, even if the answer is "I'm checking and will reply tomorrow by 10."

Can't solve it today? Acknowledge it today. Say what you are doing and when you will follow up. Silence is what slows the office down.
Out or running late? Notify your supervisor yourself, no later than your scheduled start time. Word passed through a coworker is not notice.

01 · Notes are records

Who reads what you post

Log in to each appointment when you arrive, write notes during the service, and log out when the work is done, before you move to the next stop. That is the Clements onboarding standard for PestPac Mobile, and it is what makes your time and notes accurate.

Be factual. Never record work you didn't do, a time you weren't there, or a product you didn't apply. Inaccurate records hurt the customer, the company and your license.

01 · When you disagree

Problems, not people

"Always be polite and avoid argumentative behavior, even when discussing problems or disagreements." The handbook adds that respect for supervisors and management is required. Disagreeing is fine. How you do it matters.

Never argue in front of a customer, reply-all to vent, or put something in writing you wouldn't want read aloud. Email can be forwarded and can be used in legal proceedings.
  1. 1Pause. Don't answer while angry. Re-read the message: assume a busy coworker, not a hostile one.
  2. 2Move it off email. Call or talk in person. Tone is easy to misread in writing.
  3. 3Stick to facts and what the customer needs. Offer a solution, not blame.
  4. 4Still stuck? Take it to your immediate supervisor. Then HR in writing; HR usually answers within five working days.
02 · Confidential, safe, on the record

Three rules that apply to every message

Customer information stays inside

Customer names, accounts and conditions in their homes are confidential. No photos of customer homes or comments about them on social media. Share account details only with coworkers who need them to do the job.

Company systems are company records

Email and voicemail belong to Clements and may be reviewed. Don't read messages not sent to you. Never share passwords or logins in a message. Lost or damaged phones go to your supervisor right away.

Never behind the wheel

Florida bans texting while driving and any handheld phone use in school and active work zones. Clements goes further: no calls, texts or email while driving, hands-free included. Park, then respond. Set GPS before you go.

Using your personal phone for work? The same standards apply: professional, confidential, and never while driving.
