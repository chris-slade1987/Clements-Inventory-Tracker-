02 · How we're organized

The hub-and-spoke model

All inbound calls, customer service, billing and administrative work run through the corporate office in Vero Beach, instead of each branch operating on its own. That keeps the experience consistent everywhere we work.

The hub · corporate office, Vero Beach

  • Inbound calls and customer service
  • Billing, payments and accounting
  • Routing, scheduling and new agreement entry
  • HR, payroll and benefits
  • Leadership, sales and marketing

The spokes · each branch

  • Delivers the service: technicians and routes
  • Branch manager supervises the team day to day
  • Service questions, pricing and customer complaints
  • Inventory, supplies and equipment
  • Branch safety and proper product use
What it means for you: when a customer asks about billing, scheduling or their account, the office handles it. Don't guess or promise. Pass it to the right person and let the customer know who will follow up.

02 · Chain of command and open door

Raising a concern, the right way

Clements wants to hear your complaints, problems, opinions and suggestions. Problems get solved fastest through frank, respectful conversation with the right person.

No one is penalized for expressing a point of view. Using the open door is not a nuisance to us. It's how we find and fix problems.
  1. 1Start with your immediate supervisor. Most issues are resolved here, to the satisfaction of both of you.
  2. 2You may talk with any manager or director you feel comfortable with.
  3. 3Not addressed in a reasonable time? Put it in writing to Human Resources.
  4. 4HR reviews the facts and usually responds within five working days.
